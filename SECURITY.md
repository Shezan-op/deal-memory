## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security and integrity of **DealMemory** seriously. If you discover a vulnerability, data isolation failure, prompt injection exploit, or secret leak, please report it privately.

### Responsible Disclosure Guidelines
1. **Do NOT open a public GitHub issue** containing exploit steps or active credentials.
2. Submit your findings using the [Security Vulnerability Template](file:///.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/security.md) or email our designated security contact:
   - **Security Contact**: `security@dealmemory.internal` (placeholder)
3. Please include:
   - Affected endpoint or component.
   - Proof of Concept (PoC) or reproduction steps.
   - Potential impact on tenant isolation or memory integrity.

### Our Response Commitment
- **Initial Triage**: Within 24 business hours.
- **Remediation & Patch Target**: Critical vulnerabilities within 48 hours; high within 7 days.
- **Public Disclosure**: Coordinated after a validated fix has been deployed.
