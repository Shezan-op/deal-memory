Sales AI shouldn't just summarize calls—it needs to remember what happened after you tried something.

Most CRM AI tools recommend the same generic tactics on every call. If offering a discount stalled your deal two weeks ago, a stateless LLM will still suggest another discount today.

We built DealMemory to fix this. It tracks the full feedback loop: Action → Outcome → Consolidated Memory → Better Next Action.

Powered by Hindsight, our deal intelligence engine checks past interactions, flags what stalled vs what worked, and catches conflicting budget numbers across stakeholders.
