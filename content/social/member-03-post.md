Why are we still forcing enterprise users to talk to chatbots?

Sales reps preparing for a $100k deal don't want a conversation. They need a verifiable meeting brief they can scan in 30 seconds before dialing in.

In DealMemory, we ditched the chat box for an evidence-first brief powered by Hindsight. Every recommendation cites exact historical document IDs—showing what worked, what stalled, and where stakeholders disagree.
